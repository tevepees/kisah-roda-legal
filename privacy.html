Privacy Policy

Kisah Roda Uploader · last updated 22 November 2026

This policy explains what data Kisah Roda Uploader ("the App") handles. The App is a private tool used only by the creator of Kisah Roda to publish Kisah Roda's own videos to the creator's own accounts.

1. Data we access

  • OAuth access tokens issued by TikTok, Meta (Facebook/Instagram) and Google (YouTube) after the creator approves the consent screen.
  • Basic account info needed to confirm the right account (e.g. TikTok display name / open ID, Facebook Page name and ID).
  • Status information about videos the App uploads (e.g. upload/publish status, video ID, link).

2. Data we do not collect

The App does not collect data about viewers or any other users, does not read messages, contacts or followers' data, does not use cookies or analytics, and does not sell or share data with anyone.

3. Storage & security

Tokens are stored only on the creator's own computer in files readable solely by the creator's user account. They are never published, logged, or sent anywhere except to the issuing platform's official API.

4. Retention & deletion

Tokens are kept only while the App is in use and are deleted when the App is retired. Access can be revoked at any time from the TikTok, Facebook or Google account settings, which immediately invalidates the App's access.

5. Contact

Questions or deletion requests: a message to the Kisah Roda Facebook Page or a comment on YouTube @kisahrodaid.
